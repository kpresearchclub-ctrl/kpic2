Nutrition and Food Security

  • Smart Agriculture

    • This is a proposed technology-driven farming project designed for individuals, investors, organizations, and partners interested in sustainable agriculture and agribusiness opportunities. The project will focus on pig farming, poultry farming, and crop production, with plans to add value through products such as protein-rich fortified flour, sausages, bacon, egg powder, and processed chicken meat. We are currently seeking interested partners, investors, and supporters who would like to participate in developing and establishing this innovative agricultural project. Our vision is to build a sustainable agribusiness that improves productivity, creates employment, supports food security, and generates value from locally produced agricultural products.

    Health and Nutrition

  • Umubyeyi Health and Nutrition Hub

    • Umubyeyi Health & Nutrition Hub is a research- and technology-driven initiative dedicated to improving the health and well-being of women and children. It focuses on women’s health from menarche to menopause, pregnancy and maternal care, gynecological health monitoring, child growth and development, and innovative nutrition solutions to prevent malnutrition and stunting. We welcome passionate health professionals, researchers, nutritionists, technologists, innovators, and dedicated staff members who want to contribute their skills and expertise. We are currently calling for partnerships and seeking investors, organizations, institutions, and supporters interested in advancing innovative healthcare, nutrition, research, and technology solutions. Through collaboration and investment, we aim to develop practical health technologies, strengthen nutrition production, expand research, create opportunities for talented professionals, and build sustainable solutions for healthy women, healthy children, and a healthy nation.

    Technology

  • laboratory management system

    • Exploring artificial intelligence, automation, and smart systems for tracking the student attendence eligibilty in OSCE and Inventory management. Laboratory Management System The Laboratory Management System is a technology-driven platform exploring artificial intelligence, automation, and smart digital systems to improve the management of laboratory activities. The system is designed to support efficient tracking, organization, and monitoring of laboratory resources and student participation. It focuses on student attendance and OSCE eligibility tracking, as well as laboratory inventory management, helping staff maintain accurate records, monitor available resources, reduce administrative workload, and improve efficiency. The project welcomes technology partners, developers, researchers, institutions, and investors interested in advancing smart solutions for education and laboratory management.

    Environment

  • Replast project

    • Replast Project Replast is a smart waste collection and recycling project designed to improve environmental cleanliness while turning plastic waste into valuable construction materials. Through smart collection, sorting, processing, and recycling, collected plastic will be transformed into durable pavers and building blocks that can be used in housing and infrastructure development. The project promotes clean communities, sustainable recycling, job creation, and affordable building solutions while reducing plastic pollution. We are calling for investors, partners, organizations, innovators, and community members to join us in transforming waste into opportunity and building a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable nation.

    Health

  • Smart maternal kit

    • Smart Maternal Kit Smart Maternal Kit is an innovative maternal health project designed to improve birth preparedness and support mothers throughout pregnancy, delivery, and the postnatal period. The project helps mothers understand and prepare the essential items needed for themselves and their newborns, while providing practical guidance for antenatal care, delivery preparation, and post-delivery needs. The project also aims to improve antenatal care attendance and follow-up through reminders, educational information, and organized maternal health support. We welcome healthcare professionals, researchers, technology developers, investors, organizations, and partners to collaborate with us in developing a smart, accessible solution that promotes safer motherhood and healthier newborns.
