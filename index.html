About Our Club

Innovation • Research • Entrepreneurship • Community Impact

Building Future Innovators

KP Innovation Club is a student-led platform focused on empowering young innovators through research, technology, entrepreneurship, and problem-solving.

The club supports innovative projects, startup ideas, scientific research, and leadership development among students at Kibogora Polytechnic.
