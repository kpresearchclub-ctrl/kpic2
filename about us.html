KP Innovation Club

The KP Innovation Club is a student-led organization at Kibogora Polytechnic focused on promoting innovation, research, entrepreneurship, and technology development.

We empower students to turn ideas into real solutions that solve community challenges through teamwork, creativity, and scientific thinking.
